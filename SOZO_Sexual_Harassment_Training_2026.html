Section 1: What is Sexual Harassment?

Definition, Cyprus law and types of behaviour

📖What it is

Any unwanted conduct of a sexual nature that violates dignity or creates a hostile, degrading or offensive environment.

🎯Impact not intent

"I was only joking" is not a defence. What matters is the impact on the recipient, not the intention.

🤝Who is protected

All employees regardless of gender, role or seniority. Both men and women can be victims or perpetrators.

🏢Who it covers

Colleagues, managers, patients, visitors and contractors. Harassment does not only come from within the team.

🇨🇾

Cyprus Law 205(I)/2002 (aligned with the EU Equal Treatment Directive) defines sexual harassment as any unwanted conduct of a sexual nature that violates dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading or offensive environment. It is a form of sex discrimination and is unlawful in the workplace.

⚖️Legal test

The law asks whether a reasonable person would find the behaviour offensive or distressing. The perpetrator's intent is irrelevant.

⚡Single incident is enough

Harassment does not have to be repeated to be unlawful. A single serious incident can qualify.

🗣️Verbal

Sexual comments, jokes, innuendo, intrusive personal questions or derogatory remarks about gender.

✋Physical

Unwanted touching, hugging, patting or any physical contact of a sexual nature.

✉️Written

Sexually suggestive notes, letters or printed material left in the workplace.

📱Digital

Explicit messages via email, WhatsApp or social media. Inappropriate content in work group chats.

👁️Non-verbal

Staring, leering, displaying explicit material or making sexual gestures.

🔄Persistent contact

Repeated unwanted requests for dates or romantic contact after being told no.

Section 2: Recognising Harassment

Real-world scenarios and how to apply the test

Scenario A · Persistent Unwanted Attention

A colleague repeatedly comments on your appearance and asks you out despite you saying no. You dread coming to work. This is harassment.

Scenario B · Digital

A senior staff member sends a junior employee a sexually suggestive meme via the company WhatsApp "meant as a joke." This is harassment.

Scenario C · Patient or Visitor

During a clinical session a patient makes a sexually explicit comment to a staff member. SOZO's policy covers patients and visitors too. SOZO's policy protects staff in this situation.

Scenario D · Post-Relationship Contact

After a workplace relationship ends, one person continues sending romantic messages despite being asked to stop. This becomes harassment.

⚖️The test

Ask: "Would a reasonable person find this behaviour offensive or distressing?" If yes, it likely qualifies as harassment regardless of whether the target has complained.

🙅Common misconceptions

"They never said they minded": unwanted behaviour does not require a verbal objection.

"It only happened once": a single serious incident is enough.

"We are friends": friendly relationships do not make sexual conduct acceptable at work.

💡

When in doubt, default to the more respectful option. If you are unsure whether something crosses a line, it probably does.

Section 3: Your Rights and Responsibilities

What to do and how SOZO will support you

1
Speak up if it is safe to do so
Tell the person their behaviour is unwanted and must stop. You are not obliged to do this, but it can sometimes resolve the situation quickly.
2
📝 Keep a record
Write down dates, times, location, what was said or done and any witnesses. Keep copies of messages or emails.
3
📣 Report to HR
Contact HR in confidence, verbally or in writing. hr@sozobraincenter.com
4
🛡️ You are protected
You will not be penalised for reporting in good faith. SOZO expressly prohibits retaliation.
5
💛 Support is available
If you need time or assistance, speak to HR. You will be treated with dignity throughout the process.
🛑Intervene if safe

A calm statement like "That is not appropriate" can be enough to stop behaviour in the moment.

💬Support the person

Check in privately, let them know what you saw and ask if they need help.

📣Report to HR

You can report directly to HR without the affected person's permission.

📝Document it

Write down what you witnessed with dates and details. Your account may be important in any investigation.

🛡️

You will be protected from retaliation if you report in good faith. Silence enables harassment to continue.

1
📧 Report to HR
Contact HR in person, by phone or by email. Confidential. If the complaint involves HR, report directly to the CEO.
2
⏱️ Initial assessment within 2 working days
HR will acknowledge your complaint and confirm next steps. Full confidentiality maintained.
3
🔎 Impartial investigation
Both the complainant and respondent present their accounts. Witnesses may be interviewed.

Findings communicated in writing. Where harassment is upheld, disciplinary action follows.

🔄 Follow-up
HR will check in with the complainant to confirm the situation is resolved and no retaliation has occurred.

Section 4: Consequences and Commitments

What happens when policy is violated and SOZO's guarantees

⚠️For perpetrators

Sexual harassment is a serious disciplinary matter. Consequences may include a formal written warning, demotion, suspension or termination of employment. Serious cases may be referred to Cyprus authorities.

🔍Malicious complaints

Making a false complaint in bad faith is also a disciplinary offence. An honest complaint that cannot be proved is not treated as malicious and carries no penalty.

⚖️Legal exposure

Under Cyprus Law 205(I)/2002, victims may also pursue civil claims. SOZO's policy is designed to address matters internally before they reach that point.

🛡️SOZO's commitment

SOZO Brain Center expressly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern, makes a report or participates in an investigation in good faith. Any act of retaliation is itself a serious disciplinary offence.

🤝A shared obligation

Every employee has the right to work in a safe, respectful and dignified environment. This is a legal and ethical obligation we all share, regardless of role or seniority.
